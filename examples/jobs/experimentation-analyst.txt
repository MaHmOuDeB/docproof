Experimentation Analyst — Brightleaf Health (fictional), London

About the role
Brightleaf is a subscription wellness app. You will own the analysis of our product experiments
and help teams make faster, better decisions.

What you'll do
- Design A/B tests with product managers, including power analysis and success metrics
- Analyse experiment results and write clear ship/no-ship recommendations
- Build and maintain dashboards for activation and retention KPIs
- Model data in dbt and write production-quality SQL
- Partner with engineering on event tracking and data quality
- Present insights to product leadership
- Automate recurring reporting

What you bring
- 2+ years in product or experimentation analytics
- Strong SQL; experience with dbt
- Python for analysis (pandas, statsmodels or similar)
- Experience with GA4 and Braze
- Excellent communication with non-technical stakeholders
- Nice to have: Snowflake, Airflow
